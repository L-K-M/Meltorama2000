Meltorama 2000 for macOS

Application source, warp shaders, sample artwork, and app icon edits: this
repository, released into the public domain under the Unlicense. See LICENSE.

The Mac icon is derived from Golden-retriever-dog.jpg by Karen Arnold,
available under the CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication.
Source: https://www.publicdomainpictures.net/en/view-image.php?image=31188&picture=golden-retriever-dog
License record: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Golden-retriever-dog.jpg
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
The photograph was cropped and warped using the built-in imagegen tool.
Its provenance, source/master hashes, and exact edit prompt are included in
IconProvenance.md. The original photograph and edited master are retained in
the repository's macos/Artwork/ directory.

The macOS application has no third-party runtime libraries, fonts, or encoders.
Required resources are bundled; the installed app fetches none at runtime.
It uses Apple's AppKit, SwiftUI, ImageIO, OpenGL,
AVFoundation, and the Swift standard library supplied by the operating system.
SF Symbols are displayed through Apple's system APIs under Apple's terms.

The Android application has its own dependency notices. Its runtime libraries
are not included in the macOS application.
